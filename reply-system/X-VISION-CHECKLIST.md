# @pentradev X system: vision checklist (Oct 22 2026)

**The vision.** @pentradev grows every day from two engines that run on their own. The first is replies: within
seconds of a big founder, marketer or creator posting, a sharp reply draft is ready, and those replies earn likes
and reach. The second is posts: twice a day, a post teaches founders one thing about getting found on Google and
in AI answers. The profile turns visitors into followers. Everything is measured daily, and nothing depends on
anyone remembering to do it, except posting the replies by hand (X refuses automated replies).

**Arshad's goal:** 20 to 50 new followers a day, organically. The stages below are the realistic path. Each one
is reached only when the item's number holds over 7 days, not on a single good day.

**How this file is used.** The X window's scheduled jobs (`CRON-PROMPTS.md`) work the first open item, top to
bottom. Every check re-verifies the done items, and an item that slips is reopened. An item is ticked only with
its evidence: the number, the date and where it was measured. Status: `[ ]` open, `[~]` in progress, `[x]` done.

   Google Search change (Search Central blog, Search Status Dashboard): what changed and what to do. Study posts get
   a chart of the study's own numbers. Owner: X window. **News posts live Oct 24 04:21** (bot/news.py): both official
   feeds read every 30 min, 27 existing items recorded as seen, at most one news post a day inside 12:00-23:00 UTC;
   dry run 2 of 2 pass (Search Console multimodal report, September spam update). Oct 24 08:19: a news post only uses write budget left after every remaining slot post of the month (Nov: 60 slots = $0.90 of the $1 cap). Charts deferred: X's rate card has no media-upload price, one developer reports uploads billed as post creations, and an image post would break the $0.05 daily cap

3. **Paid reach: an X Ads followers test** once posts are back above about 20 views, capped, to measure the real cost
   per follow. Owner: Arshad approves the spend once; X window picks the post and reads the result.
4. **Measured daily** from the summary (post views, follows). Owner: X window.

## Decisions and evidence

- Oct 22: replies that agree with and sharpen the author's point win (2,044 and 1,344 views); auditing replies get
  20-40 views. Under 110 characters: median 195 views; over 160: 36.
- Oct 22: posts research (43 sources): a teaching post earns about 4x the bookmarks per like of a milestone
  post; X can label an account "llm_slop" and drop its posts from For You for 30 days; personal accounts out-reach
  brand accounts.
- Oct 22: growth research (about 140 sources, X's published code): replies alone at 6-12 a day give 0.1-2 follows
  a day (measured funnels: 0.13-0.68 follows per 1K impressions; visit-to-follow 4.7%). The ranked levers are
  C0, A11 with A10, a reciprocal in-niche roster, B and A9. **Premium+: no.** X calls the boost "a slight
  preference", no reply-level test exists, and even a 2.6x lift adds about 0.3-5 follows a day for $22-32 more a
  month. Skip the gold check, faster replies, links in replies and engagement bait.
